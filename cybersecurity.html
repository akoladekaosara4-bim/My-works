Cybersecurity Awareness

Stay Safe Online!

Welcome to our Cybersecurity Awareness Project. This website provides useful information on how to protect yourself from online threats and cyber attacks.

1. What is Cybersecurity?

Cybersecurity is the practice of protecting computers, networks, systems, and personal information from digital attacks, unauthorized access, and cyber threats.

2. Common Cybersecurity Threats

  • Phishing attacks
  • Malware and computer viruses
  • Identity theft
  • Password hacking
  • Online scams

3. How to Stay Safe Online

  • Use strong and unique passwords.
  • Never share your passwords or PINs.
  • Avoid clicking suspicious links.
  • Keep your software and applications updated.
  • Enable two-factor authentication.
  • Avoid sharing sensitive personal information online.

4. Why Cybersecurity Matters

Cybersecurity helps protect personal data, financial information, and digital identities. It prevents cybercrime, reduces security risks, and promotes safer internet usage.

5. Cybersecurity Awareness Tips

  • Think before you click.
  • Verify suspicious emails and messages.
  • Use trusted websites when making online transactions.
  • Log out of accounts when using shared devices.
  • Back up important files regularly.

Stay Protected!

Staying safe online begins with awareness. Be responsible, stay alert, and protect your digital identity.
